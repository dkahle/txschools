2022-23 TAPR Advanced Data Download

This advanced download includes Numerators, Denominators & Rates used in the 2022-23 Texas Academic Performance Report and additional data on the TPRS system. The only download format available is comma-delimited (*.csv) due to the large size of the files. The data download can exceed 2000 columns. See Additional Download Information for student group naming conventions, download formats, and general information.
