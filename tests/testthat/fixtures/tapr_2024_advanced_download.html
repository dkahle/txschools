2023-24 TAPR Advanced Data Download

This advanced download includes Numerators, Denominators & Rates used in the 2023-24 Texas Academic Performance Report and additional data on the TPRS system. The only download format available is comma-delimited (*.csv) due to the large size of the files. The data download can exceed 2000 columns. See Additional Download Information for group naming conventions, download formats, and general information.

TAPR Advanced Download Reference of Data Elements:
  1. Click on Data Elements next to each category of the TAPR Download to get a variable listing in HTML, PDF, or Excel.
  2. TAPR Data Dictionary (PDF) statutorily-required data and Additional TPRS Data (PDF) found on the comprehensive TPRS HTML system.
